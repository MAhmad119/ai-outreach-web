About Us

OutreachAI is a lightweight, client-side micro-SaaS tool designed to help freelancers, sales teams, and marketers instantly draft high-converting cold messages without heavy subscriptions or backend setup.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays 100% private. OutreachAI runs entirely in your web browser. No names, companies, or generated messages are sent to any external server or stored in any database.
